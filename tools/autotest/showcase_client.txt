# SHOWCASE (client, right window): the friend's side of the tour.
on_fail continue
caption Fallout 1 co-op v0.6 - CLIENT (right window)
keys space 4
wait_for 90000 Coop-stage: main menu reached
wait 5000
key l
wait 3500
key enter
wait_for 30000 LOADSAVE: Total load data read
wait 6000
caption CLIENT: loads its own save (its own character) and joins the host
coop_join 127.0.0.1
wait_for 120000 client: HELLO_ACK received
wait_for 60000 client: map
wait 6000
caption Joined: the host's world appears here
wait 14000
caption The client walks too
click 400 280
wait 5000
click 260 240
wait 6000
caption NEW: the client gives its character perks and traits - the host uses them in combat
perk bonus_ranged_damage 2
perk sniper 1
perk more_criticals 1
perk action_boy 2
perk toughness 1
trait fast_shot finesse
char_changed
wait 6000
caption Combat: the client attacks with its own character
wait_for 120000 Coop: received COMBAT_BEGIN
wait_for 120000 myTurnActive=1
wait 3000
caption Called shot: the client aims at the EYES
aim on
called_shot eyes
cursor attack
hover_enemy
wait 2000
click_here
wait 8000
aim off
caption ...and a normal shot
cursor attack
hover_enemy
wait 2000
click_here
wait 8000
key space
wait 12000
caption Cutscenes: the client plays the host's cutscene too
wait_for 120000 playing the host's cutscene
wait 8000
caption Reach animations and conversations are mirrored here
wait 60000

wait_for 150000 received GAME_OVER
wait 9000
keys space 8
wait 12000
key l
wait 3500
key enter
wait_for 30000 LOADSAVE: Total load data read
caption NEW: after loading a save the client rejoins by itself
wait_for 120000 rejoining
